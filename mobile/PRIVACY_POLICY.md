# Privacy Policy — Umrah Companion

**Last updated:** [fill in date of publishing]

Umrah Companion is developed and operated by Alzakwaan Tours & Travels Pvt Ltd
("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles and how.

## Information we collect

**Customize-package enquiries.** When you fill in the "Customize Package" form (city,
number of travellers, nights, preferred month, hotel category, and any notes you add),
that information is sent to our server so our staff can see and follow up on your
enquiry, and is also used to prefill a WhatsApp message you can choose to send us.

**Nothing else is collected.** The app does not require an account or login. We do not
use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

## Information stored only on your device

Your language, theme (light/dark), and Urdu layout (RTL/LTR) preferences, and your
progress ticking off items in the First-Time Guide, Packing Checklist, and Vaccine
Checklist, are stored locally on your device and are never sent to us.

## How enquiry information is used

Customize-package enquiries are used solely to respond to your request for a quote or
booking. We do not sell or share this information with third parties, other than the
service providers necessary to deliver the trip you're enquiring about (e.g. airlines,
hotels) once you proceed with a booking.

## WhatsApp and phone contact

Tapping "Enquire on WhatsApp" or "Call the office" opens WhatsApp or your phone's dialer
with our number prefilled. Messages you send us that way are subject to WhatsApp's own
privacy policy as well as this one.

## Content shown in the app

Umrah guide content, duas, checklists, package listings, news, and FAQ answers are
managed by our staff and don't involve your personal data.

## Data retention

Enquiry submissions are retained as long as needed to respond to your request and for
our business records, and can be deleted on request.
